Motivation

Why this project exists

Physics case

  • The FASER spectrometer (IFT + three SCT stations in dipole fields) measures ~100 GeV–TeV muon tracks; station-level misalignment directly biases momentum and decay-vertex reconstruction.
  • Alignment needs tracks that cross all four stations: a local tracklet per station must first be associated into a global route before any global fit can run.
  • At millimetre-scale misalignment the association itself fails first — you cannot align with tracks you never built.

Why machine learning

  • Association is a discrete pattern-recognition problem on a physical candidate graph; learned geometric models may widen the capture range beyond χ²-only matching.
  • An ML backbone plus an iterative associate → fit → align → refit loop targets global alignment without truth association.
Spectrometer layout and the three active alignment degrees of freedom of the IFT (station 0).
This project’s claim to evidence: an end-to-end real Calypso/Acts physical chain (no coordinate-shift surrogates), strict source-disjoint train/validation governance, and a sealed test bank with frozen, hash-pinned contracts.

Alignment update (current iteration)

  • Truth-fixed multi-source finite-difference (FD) Jacobian from ±probe refits; weighted least-squares local step; train fits, validation is held out.
  • Payload semantics verified against Calypso source: per-station [dx, dy, dz, Rx, Ry, Rz], global T·Rz·Ry·Rx; both segment refit and Acts surfaces follow the payload.
  • Local segment q/p is not a usable measurement → propagation always mode 0 (enforced at ~25 checkpoints).

Statistical governance

  • Splits are original-xAOD-file disjoint: 10 train / 8 validation sources; sealed multi-direction test (2 sources, 19 events) frozen 2026-08-12, hash-pinned, read only by two sanctioned historical scans.
  • Model selection, calibration, thresholds: validation-only. 85 contract JSONs audited: no test access outside the frozen scans.
  • Full test suite: 163 passed.

Result A — Association on Large Translations Fails First

Expanded dx/dy corpus, validation-only frozen controls
  • Raw candidate graph keeps 100% truth-chain recall at every magnitude — the failure is downstream, in association.
  • V2 BCE route query passes the gate at 0–1 mm (nominal 0.916 / 0.967 / 0.044); every model fails at ≥ 5 mm (V2 5 mm: eff 0.756, purity 0.946, fake 0.072).
  • MLP baseline fails the fake gate even at nominal (0.063).
  • V3 structured margin: capture = 0 at all six magnitudes → confirmed dead end, suspended.

Result A′ — Physical Refit Capture Boundary (Truth-Fixed)

25-point scan: 9 magnitudes × 3 directions, full re-reconstruction per point
outputs/mc24_muon_fasernu_physical_capture_scan_v1/capture_scan_summary.json
  • With truth-fixed pairs, a one-shot linear update on the real refit chain captures all 0.1 mm trials (3/3) and fails all ≥ 1 mm trials (0/3) under the strict 0.01 mm criterion; ≥ 500 mm loses fit rank entirely.
  • Sanity anchor: station-3 +1 mm physical closure recovered to max error 4.9 × 10−13 mm over 27 truth-matched pairs — the chain itself is exact; capture is limited by linearization + association range.
  • Configuration-specific control result (χ² gate, seed windows), not a universal detector tolerance.
The 0.1–1 mm association boundary above motivates iterating around a moving anchor rather than demanding one giant linear step.

Summary

What is established, what is not, and the decision in flight

Established

  • End-to-end real misalignment chain (payload → refit → Acts) — exact to 10−13 mm at truth-fixed closure.
  • On translations ≥ 5 mm: association fails first while candidate truth recall stays 1.0; no Transformer capture-range expansion shown (honest negative).
  • IFT Ry: recoverable to < 1 mrad at ±60 mrad; rotation is not the association bottleneck.
  • Multi-DoF iteration-0: full-rank, validated linear response (held-out χ² −99.6%); residual dx/dy bias is source-dependent systematics (5–6× stat σ), with dx–Ry correlation 0.85.

Not yet established

  • Multi-DoF joint recovery at frozen tolerances — iteration-1 closure decides (in production now).
  • Truth-free (unknown-association) alignment update; iterative loop convergence.
  • Raw candidate coverage sufficiency (0→1 losses); 6-DoF, layer/module alignment; anything on real data.
