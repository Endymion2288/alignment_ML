## 1. Hypothesis and coordinate contract

HYPOTHESIS: the complete raw-fit → native nominal plane → same fixed global-z plane Jacobian preserves the raw fitted 4D covariance, including position/slope cross blocks. Repair changes covariance only; fitted parameters, cluster cuts/selection and geometry remain fixed.

Raw fit is `(x_center,y_center,tx,ty)` at fitter zCenter, mm and dimensionless slopes. At zFit, raw position is `(x+tx*dz,y+ty*dz,zFit)`. Native order is `(loc1,loc2,phi,theta,q/p)`; nominal curvilinear loc1/loc2 are zero. Actual U/V/n axes come from `associatedSurface().transform()` in Athena24.0.41, not inferred station meanings. The nominal plane is held fixed for local perturbations. A perturbed raw line is intersected with that plane. Position derivatives are U/V dot `(dx+dz*dtx,dy+dz*dty,0)`; phi derivatives are `(-ty,tx)/(tx²+ty²)`; theta derivatives are `(tx,ty)/(r*(1+r²))`.

New export explicitly returns the line at nominal fixed zRef: `x_ref=x+(zRef-z)*px/pz`, likewise y. Its native positional Jacobian is `(U_x-tx*U_z,V_x-tx*V_z)` and corresponding y row. Raw-position export on a tilted native plane is a different surface contract. This distinction supplements WB90; historical artifacts are not changed. At nominal z, values agree, but uncertainties need not.

At dz≠0, composed Jacobian must equal the raw lever-arm matrix T (identity plus T[0,2]=T[1,3]=dz), not identity. Covariance must be `T C Tᵀ`. Existing q/p=+1e-5 MeV^-1 and dummy variance=5e-6 MeV^-2 are preserved, and are not presented as physical momentum calibration. Exactly zero transverse slope makes phi undefined: fail explicitly, do not add a numerical floor.
